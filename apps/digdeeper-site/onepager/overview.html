Company overview

Unclaimed funds recovery. Paid only when you are paid.

D.I.G. Deeper LLC locates unclaimed funds held by government agencies and institutions, identifies the rightful owners, and handles the recovery from start to finish.

Who we are

D.I.G. Deeper LLC is an asset recovery firm based in Jacksonville, Florida, working nationwide. We research public records to find funds that have been separated from their owners and do the work of getting that money back to the people it belongs to.

How does money go unclaimed? Billions of dollars sit with agencies, courts and institutions across the country, owed to people who moved, changed names, passed away, or never received notice. The agencies holding these funds do not go looking for the owners.

What we do

  1. We research. Our team works through public records and agency data to identify unclaimed funds and establish who they belong to.
  2. We find the owner. We locate the rightful owner or their heirs and confirm we have the right person before anything else happens.
  3. We handle the claim. We prepare the documentation, coordinate signatures and notarization, file with the holding agency, and follow up until the funds are released.
  4. You get paid, then we do. Our fee comes out of the recovered funds after they are released. Nothing is owed before that.

Our fee

  • Contingency only. A percentage of what is recovered, paid only after recovery.
  • No upfront cost. We cover the research, filing and follow-up. You never pay for anything along the way.
  • No recovery, no fee. If nothing is recovered, you owe nothing. The risk is ours.
  • Set in writing first. Our percentage and every other term is stated in a written agreement you sign before any work begins. Where state law limits recovery fees, our fee complies.

No pressure and no surprises. Take time to think it over or have someone you trust look at the agreement. The funds are not going anywhere while you decide.
