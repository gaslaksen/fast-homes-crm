Company overview

Florida surplus funds recovery. Paid only when you are paid.

D.I.G. Deeper LLC finds former property owners and their heirs who are owed surplus funds from a Florida foreclosure or tax deed sale, and handles the claim with the county clerk from start to finish.

Who we are

D.I.G. Deeper LLC is a Florida limited liability company based in Jacksonville. We work with people who are owed money held by Florida county clerks and who often do not know it exists.

What are surplus funds? When a Florida property is sold at a foreclosure or tax deed auction for more than what was owed, the extra money is surplus. It belongs to the former owner or their heirs and sits with the county clerk until it is claimed.

What we do

  1. Find you. The clerk's notice usually goes to the old property address. We research public records to locate the rightful claimants.
  2. Confirm the claim. We verify the case, the amount the clerk holds, and who has the legal right to it, including heirs.
  3. Handle the paperwork. We prepare the claim, arrange notarized signatures, file with the clerk and follow it to disbursement.
  4. You get paid, then we do. Our fee comes only out of funds actually recovered, after the clerk releases them.

How the fee works

  • Contingency only. A percentage of what is recovered, paid only after recovery.
  • No advance fee. Nothing up front, and no costs passed to you along the way.
  • No recovery, no fee. If the clerk releases nothing, you owe nothing.
  • Capped by Florida law. Florida limits what anyone may be paid to recover clerk-held surplus. Our fee never exceeds that cap.
  • In writing first. The exact fee and every other form of consideration is stated in the agreement you sign before we do any work.

You do not need an attorney or a company like ours to claim surplus funds. You can file directly with the county clerk yourself at no charge.
