Who we are

D.I.G. Deeper LLC is a Florida limited liability company based in Jacksonville. We help former property owners and their heirs recover surplus funds held by Florida county clerks.

What are surplus funds?

When a Florida property is sold at a foreclosure or tax deed auction for more than what was owed, the extra money is called surplus funds. It belongs to the former owner or their heirs, and it sits with the county clerk until someone claims it.

What we do

  1. We find you

    The clerk's notice usually goes to the property address, and the former owner has already moved. We research public records to locate the people the money belongs to.

  2. We confirm the claim

    We verify the case, the amount the clerk is holding, and who has the legal right to claim it. If there are heirs, we help sort out who must sign.

  3. We handle the paperwork

    We prepare the claim, arrange notarized signatures, file with the clerk, and follow the case through to disbursement.

  4. You get paid, then we do

    Our fee comes out of the funds that are actually recovered, after the clerk releases them. Nothing changes hands before that.

How the fee works

  • Contingency only. We are paid a percentage of what is recovered, and only after it is recovered.
  • No advance fee. You never pay us anything up front, and we never ask you to cover costs along the way.
  • No recovery, no fee. If the clerk releases nothing, you owe nothing.
  • Capped by Florida law. Florida limits what anyone can be paid to recover surplus funds held by a county clerk. Our fee never exceeds that cap.
  • In writing before we start. The exact fee, and every other form of consideration, is stated in the agreement you sign before we do any work.

You do not need an attorney or a company like ours to claim surplus funds. You can file directly with the county clerk yourself at no charge. We are here for people who would rather have someone find the case, handle the paperwork and see it through.
